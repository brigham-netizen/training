About Brigham Redd

Entrepreneur · Founder · Idaho Falls, Idaho

Brigham Redd is an entrepreneur based in Idaho Falls, Idaho. Redd is the founder of SettleSavvy, an AI platform that ranks neighborhoods by what matters to each buyer; Lift Off Realty, an AI-powered real estate and property management company serving eastern Idaho; Palisades Vacation Getaways, a set of cabins, tiny houses and glamping tents in Irwin, Idaho; and Grand Design, a flooring store in Jackson, Wyoming.

Redd started SettleSavvy around a gap seen again and again in home buying: people can compare houses easily, but not the places those houses sit in. SettleSavvy treats neighborhood fit as the first question rather than the last.

Redd earned a Bachelor of Science in Business Management from Capella University in 2019.
